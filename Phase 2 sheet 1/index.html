Q. 03 / 17

What is TCS NQT?

  • Single integrated test — ~190 minutes (~3 hours)
  • ONE exam decides three possible offers: Ninja (₹3.36 LPA) · Digital (₹7 LPA) · Prime (₹9+ LPA)
  • Score valid 2 years, accepted by 1,700+ companies
  • No negative marking — attempt every single question
  • AI-proctored · Languages allowed: C, C++, Java, Python, Perl
⚠️ The Foundation section cutoff decides whether your Advanced section performance even gets considered for Digital / Prime.

How each band is cleared — and the cutoffs

BAND ELIGIBILITY
MOST COMMON

Ninja

Clear only the Foundation section (Numerical + Verbal + Reasoning) + 1 interview round.

~70–75% of all TCS NQT hires land here.

HIGHER BAR

Digital

Clear the Foundation cutoff AND perform well in Advanced (Adv. Aptitude + Coding).

One extra interview round on top of Ninja's process.

TOP TIER

Prime

Highest bar across both sections + strong coding performance + extended technical interview.

CUTOFF ESTIMATES
TCS does not officially publish cutoffs. These are community/student-reported estimates and vary per cycle — treat as a target, not a guarantee.

Q. 06 / 17

Exam mechanics & golden rules

TIMING
  • Foundation section — 76 minutes total
  • Advanced section — 115 minutes total
  • Within Advanced, Advanced Coding alone gets 90 minutes
MARKING
  • No negative marking — never leave anything blank, always mark a best guess
NAVIGATION
  • Section switching — not allowed (e.g. can't go back from Advanced to Foundation)
  • Sub-section switching — not allowed (e.g. can't jump back from Verbal to Numerical)
  • Candidates are allowed to revisit already-answered questions within the current sub-section
PROCTORING
  • Browser tab shifting — not allowed, exam ends immediately
  • Video & audio monitored by AI (for candidates testing from home)
  • One teacher proctors ~20 students
  • Partial marking in coding — submit even a partial solution that passes some test cases
  • On-screen calculator + rough sheet only — no physical material at most centres
  • Write your coding I/O template on the rough sheet before starting Question 1
  • All questions mandatory, difficulty fixed (non-adaptive) within a section

Coding: the 80% rule

~80% of coding questions revolve around just these patterns.

Arrays

  • 2nd largest element
  • Reverse array
  • Find duplicates
  • Move zeroes
  • Rotate array
  • Prefix sum / subarray sum

Strings

  • Palindrome check
  • Reverse string
  • Anagram
  • Character frequency
  • Substring problems

Numbers

  • Prime check
  • Sieve of Eratosthenes
  • Fibonacci
  • Factorial
  • Armstrong number
  • GCD & LCM

Pattern Printing

  • Star patterns
  • Number patterns

Sort & Search

  • Bubble sort
  • Insertion sort
  • Binary search
Q. 12 / 17

⚠️ Critical: TCS iON I/O format

This is different from LeetCode. You must practise command-line style input/output — the exam platform will not hold your hand like an online judge does.
  • Memorise your input/output template before exam day
  • Write your template on the rough sheet before starting Question 1
  • Practise on: OnlineGDB (command-line arguments) and the official TCS iON practice tests (4 free tests after registering)
